Online store FAQ

How much does it cost to build an online store?

From $900 for the base, and a working shop with payments, delivery and an admin panel comes to roughly $2,000–$2,500. Ukrainian agencies typically charge from $2,500 upwards for the same scope.

Can I add products myself?

Yes, with the $300 admin panel. It is a separate area where you add products and change prices, photos and descriptions without contacting us. Without it, we make the edits as part of support.

Can the store take card payments?

Yes — $400 for full payments: card, Google Pay, Apple Pay, money straight to your account. For selling abroad there is a foreign payment provider at $600. If you do not need to take money on the site yet, the simple option is $100: the order lands in a Telegram bot.

Do you integrate delivery services?

Yes. Nova Poshta is $200: the customer picks their branch on the site and the waybill is created automatically, so you never type addresses by hand.

Do you build on Shopify or a website builder?

No, we write the store in our own code. That means no monthly platform fee, no template limits, and the site stays entirely yours. The honest flip side: a builder is cheaper to start with, and if that is genuinely what you need, we will say so.

How many products can the store hold?

There is no technical limit. What affects the price is not the product count itself but copywriting (+$2 per description) and the need for filters — past roughly twenty items they stop being optional.
